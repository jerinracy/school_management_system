## [Unreleased]
### Added
- **Task 11**: Exam Setup & Mark Entry (`apps/exams`).
  - Core Models:
    - `ExamType`: Institutional exam categories (Term, Class Test, Model Test, Final, Send-Up, Board Mock) with weightage for final grade compilation.
    - `Exam`: Term-bound examination cycle with registration deadlines, date windows, multi-class assignment, and strict status progression (`draft`, `scheduled`, `in_progress`, `marks_entry`, `tabulating`, `published`, `archived`).
    - `ExamSubject`: Subject-level exam specifications with maximum/pass mark definitions for CQ (creative/written), MCQ, and practical components, pass policy (`all_components`, `overall_aggregate`, `theory_practical_separate`), full paper marks, and combined 1st/2nd paper grouping.
    - `ExamRoutine`: Schedule slots per exam subject with date, start/end time, room/venue assignment, invigilators, and instructions.
    - `ExamEligibilityPolicy`: Rules-based student eligibility criteria (minimum attendance percentage, clearance of fee dues).
    - `StudentExamEligibility`: Evaluated eligibility records per student with clearance flags, override capabilities, and automated admit card issuance.
    - `AdmitCard`: Unique barcoded/numbered examination hall pass linked to eligible students, including download timestamps.
    - `SeatPlan`: Physical seating allocations linking student, room, bench number, and seat position with support for cross-class interleaving.
    - `MarkEntry`: Fine-grained student mark records storing creative, mcq, and practical score components, total calculated marks, absent/withheld flags, and lock state.
    - `MarkEntryLog`: Full audit trail recording all mark entry and modification events, old/new score snapshots, user, IP, and reason.
    - `GraceMarkRule`: Institutional policy for borderline grade/pass grace mark allocation.
    - `ReScrutinyRequest`: Formal student/guardian re-scrutiny workflow tracking requested subjects, payment status, review outcome, and mark revisions.
  - Service Layer (`apps/exams/services`):
    - `eligibility_service.py`: Automated student eligibility evaluator computing attendance percentage against `DailyAttendance` records, verifying fee clearance hooks, and issuing admit cards.
    - `seat_plan_service.py`: Algorithmic seat plan generator supporting sequential seating and cross-class/cross-section interleaving to prevent cheating.
    - `admit_card_service.py`: TrueType Bangla PDF generator using ReportLab and `Kalpurush.ttf` producing hall passes with exam routine schedule, student details, and signature blocks.
    - `mark_entry_service.py`: Mark entry engine with component-level score validation, teacher assignment permission verification, single/bulk entry saves, lock/unlock workflow, audit trail logging, and openpyxl-powered Excel template export/import.
  - REST API ViewSets (`apps/exams/views.py`):
    - `ExamTypeViewSet` & `ExamViewSet`: Lifecycle management, routine generation, seat planning, and admit card generation.
    - `ExamSubjectViewSet`: Mark entry progress tracking, teacher authorization check, bulk mark saving, locking, and unlocking.
    - `ExamRoutineViewSet`, `SeatPlanViewSet`, `AdmitCardViewSet`: CRUD and PDF download endpoints.
    - `MarkEntryViewSet`: Entry querying, Excel template export, and spreadsheet import.
    - `ReScrutinyRequestViewSet` & `GraceMarkRuleViewSet`: Administrative re-scrutiny and grace mark review.
- **Task 10**: Attendance Engine & Configurable Rules (`apps/attendance`).
  - Core Models:
    - `AttendancePolicy`: Granular, versioned attendance rule configuration supporting shift/branch/class scoping, flexible start/end times, on-time grace minutes, late/half-day thresholds, early leave threshold, minimum work duration for staff, multi-punch modes (`first_last`, `every_pair`, `single`), and configurable Friday/Saturday weekend defaults.
    - `PolicyAssignment`: Binds policies to specific branches, shifts, or class levels with priority ordering.
    - `AttendanceEvent`: Normalized, deduplicated check-in/out event records linked to biometric hardware punches or mobile check-ins.
    - `DailyAttendance`: Idempotent daily roll record per student/staff member storing evaluated status (`present`, `late`, `half_day`, `absent`, `holiday`, `weekend`, `leave`), first-in/last-out timestamps, total minutes, late minutes, early leave minutes, and manual teacher/principal overrides.
    - `PeriodAttendance`: Subject- and period-level classroom roll call for secondary/higher-secondary sections, linked to `RoutineSlot` and recorded by the subject teacher.
    - `AttendanceCorrection`: Formal change workflow allowing teachers/staff to submit correction requests with audit reason, reviewed and approved/rejected by school administrators.
    - `MobileCheckin`: High-precision GPS mobile check-in logging coordinates, accuracy, geofence validation result, distance from campus, and optional verification selfie.
  - Service Layer & Rule Engine (`apps/attendance/services`):
    - `rule_engine.py`: Pure, deterministic evaluation engine `evaluate_daily_status()` with zero DB side effects; evaluates arrival and departure against policy thresholds, enforces staff minimum duration, and respects institutional holidays, configured weekends, and approved staff leave.
    - `geofence_service.py`: Server-side Haversine distance calculator and geofence boundary validator (`validate_geofence()`).
    - `punch_processor.py`: Automated background ingestion pipeline `process_raw_punches()` converting unprocessed Task 09 `RawPunch` records into `AttendanceEvent`s with in-batch and database 60-second duplicate punch suppression, triggering automatic daily attendance recalculation.
    - `attendance_service.py`: Policy resolver (`resolve_effective_policy()`), daily attendance recalculator (`update_daily_attendance_for_person()`, `recompute_daily_attendance()`), automated end-of-day absentee marker (`mark_daily_absentees_for_date()`), manual bulk attendance intake, and correction decision workflow.
  - Background Tasks (`apps/attendance/tasks.py`):
    - `process_unprocessed_raw_punches_task`: Celery worker task processing batches of unprocessed hardware punches.
    - `mark_daily_absentees_task`: Scheduled EOD Celery task marking absentees, holidays, and weekends for unrecorded students and staff.
  - REST API ViewSets (`apps/attendance/views.py`):
    - `AttendancePolicyViewSet`: CRUD endpoints with `/simulate/` testing sandbox action for dry-run policy evaluation.
    - `DailyAttendanceViewSet`: Daily roll viewing, date/status filtering, `/bulk_manual/` teacher intake, and `/recompute/` admin trigger.
    - `PeriodAttendanceViewSet`: Period-by-period roll-call viewing and `/bulk_submit/` teacher intake.
    - `AttendanceCorrectionViewSet`: Correction request submission and administrative `/decide/` approval/rejection action.
    - `AttendanceEventViewSet`: Read-only audit stream of normalized biometric and mobile events.
    - `MobileCheckinViewSet`: GPS-validated intake endpoint for mobile staff check-in.
- **Task 09**: Biometric & Attendance Device Management (`apps/devices`).
  - Core Models:
    - `Device`: Hardware terminal registry supporting fingerprint, facial recognition, iris, palm vein, RFID/NFC smart cards, QR, and virtual simulators across vendors (ZKTeco, Hikvision, Suprema, eSSL, Custom). Unique serial number constraint per institution, API token and HMAC secret support.
    - `DeviceGroup` & `DeviceGroupMember`: Logical perimeter groupings of terminals partitioned by audience (`all`, `students`, `staff`, `drivers`).
    - `DeviceUserMap`: Binds device hardware PINs to student or staff identities.
    - `BiometricConsent`: Enforces child privacy (`AGENTS.md §A3 Rule 4`) with mandatory guardian authorization for students, version tracking, and instant decommissioning on withdrawal.
    - `BiometricEnrollment`: Safe enrollment tracking storing only metadata (PIN, method, device, consent) with absolute zero raw biometric images or templates stored.
    - `DeviceCommand`: Asynchronous hardware command queue for enrollment, deletion, reboot, time sync, and log clearing.
    - `DeviceHealthLog`: Periodic diagnostic telemetry and clock drift tracking.
    - `RawPunch`: Immutable, append-only log of raw punches. Unique constraint on `(device, raw_user_id, device_time)` guarantees idempotent ingestion without duplicate records.
  - Multi-Protocol Hardware Adapters (`apps/devices/adapters`):
    - `AdapterRegistry`: Dynamic resolution of adapters by vendor and protocol.
    - `ZKTecoPushAdapter`: Handles ZKTeco ADMS push protocol, parsing tab-delimited ATTLOG lines and formatting user commands.
    - `GenericHTTPAdapter`: Modern JSON webhook intake with HMAC-SHA256 signature verification.
    - `FakeDeviceAdapter`: High-throughput simulator for automated testing and demo queues.
    - Hardware protocol stubs for Hikvision ISAPI, Suprema BioStar 2, and eSSL Pull SDK.
  - Ingestion & Service Layer (`apps/devices/services`):
    - `ingest_raw_punches`: High-throughput punch intake resolving users via `DeviceUserMap`, routing unknown scans to `unmapped` review queue, updating device online status and calculating clock drift.
    - `resolve_unmapped_punch`: Re-links unmapped punch to student/staff, creates permanent user mapping, and resolves other pending punches with matching PIN.
    - `record_biometric_consent` & `withdraw_biometric_consent`: Strict guardian consent validation and hardware user decommissioning.
    - `enroll_user_on_device`: Validates active consent and queues hardware enrollment command.
  - REST API & Hardware Endpoints:
    - Endpoints for Terminals, Groups, User Maps, Consents, Enrollments, Commands, and Raw Punches.
    - Unmapped punch review queue with `/resolve/` action.
    - Dedicated root `/iclock/` routes for physical ZKTeco ADMS terminals: `/iclock/cdata` (heartbeat & ATTLOG push), `/iclock/getrequest` (command polling), `/iclock/devicecmd` (command acknowledgement).
    - Generic Webhook push intake at `/api/v1/devices/push/webhook/`.
  - Management Command:
    - `simulate_punches`: CLI tool simulating realistic check-in/out scans and unmapped queues for demo and verification.
- **Task 08**: Staff & HR Management (Teachers, Employees, Designations, Leave, Assignments) (`apps/staff`).
  - Core Models:
    - `Department`: Academic and administrative departments with optional Head of Department link.
    - `Designation`: Hierarchical job ranks (`rank_order`) categorized by `role_category` (`teacher`, `admin`, `support`, `driver`, `security`).
    - `Staff`: Multi-tenant master employee directory with institution-scoped `employee_id` sequence (`EMP-YYYY-XXXXX`), normalized phone numbers, NID, MPO status flag and index number, TIN, joining/confirmation dates, and portal user account linkage.
    - `StaffEducation`, `StaffDocument`, and `StaffIDCard`: Academic credentials, verification documents, and official badges.
  - Leave Management Subsystem:
    - `LeaveType`: Institutional leave categories (CL, ML, MAT, EL, SPL) with yearly quotas, carry-forward limits, and gender restrictions.
    - `LeaveBalance`: Per-staff, per-session annual quota ledger calculating available remaining days.
    - `LeaveRequest`: Application lifecycle (`pending` → `recommended` → `approved` / `rejected` / `cancelled`) with concurrency-safe `select_for_update` balance ledger modifications and date overlap prevention.
  - Teaching Workload & Assignments:
    - `TeacherAssignment`: Explicit instructor-to-subject/section mapping with periods per week and primary teacher flag.
    - Academics Integration: Added nullable `teacher_staff` foreign keys on `Section`, `RoutineSlot`, and `SubstituteAssignment` models for seamless transition from user-based references.
    - `WorkloadSummarySelector`: Computed metrics on assigned periods, weekly routine slots, unique sections, and subjects.
  - REST API (`/api/v1/staff/`):
    - Endpoints for Department, Designation, Staff CRUD, `/me/`, `/workload/`, `/create-portal-account/`.
    - Leave Types, Leave Balances (`/initialize-session/`), and Leave Requests (`/recommend/`, `/approve/`, `/reject/`, `/cancel/`).
    - Teacher Assignments CRUD.
- **Task 07**: Guardian Portal & Student-Guardian Relationship (`apps/guardians`).
  - Core Models:
    - `Guardian`: Multi-tenant master guardian directory storing normalized contact numbers (`+8801XXXXXXXXX`), NID (10/13/17 digits), occupation, annual income, addresses, emergency contact flag, and optional User linkage for parent portal access. Unique active phone constraint per tenant.
    - `StudentGuardian`: Central relationship mapping connecting students to guardians with permission flags (`is_primary`, `can_pickup`, `receives_sms`, `financial_responsible`) and relationship types (Father, Mother, Brother, Sister, Uncle, Aunt, Grandparent, Legal Guardian, Other). Unique student-guardian constraint.
    - `Family`: Household grouping entity linking siblings and primary contacts under a single tenant-scoped family code (`FAM-YYYY-XXXXX`).
    - `AuthorizedPickup`: Third-party pickup authorization records with OTP-based parent verification workflow (`otp_verified`, `otp_verified_at`).
  - Business Services:
    - `guardian_service.py`: Automated phone normalization, guardian creation and update, portal User provisioning with secure password generation and SMS dispatch via `SMSService`, atomic primary guardian linkage and synchronization with Student emergency contact info, and duplicate guardian merging.
    - `family_service.py`: Sibling discovery engine searching across shared contact phone numbers and guardian NIDs with automated candidate matching and Family grouping.
    - `pickup_service.py`: Designated pickup person registration with notification dispatch and 6-digit OTP verification challenge via Redis cache.
  - Security & Permissions:
    - Centralized `IsGuardianOfStudent` permission class ensuring guardian users only access records belonging to their linked children.
    - DRF ViewSets with tenant scoping and role-based permissions (`guardians.view`, `guardians.manage`).
  - REST API (`/api/v1/guardians/`):
    - Guardian CRUD, `/link-student/`, `/unlink-student/`, `/create-portal-account/`, `/merge-duplicates/`, `/sibling-suggestions/`.
    - `/my-children/` endpoint aggregating linked wards, active enrollment, section roll, and parent permissions.
    - Family CRUD and `/link-siblings/`.
    - Authorized Pickup CRUD, `/send-otp/`, and `/verify-otp/`.
- **Task 06**: Student Management (Profile, Enrollment, Documents, ID Cards, Promotion, Bulk Import, Bangla PDF) (`apps/students`, `apps/core/pdf`).
  - Core Models:
    - `Student`: Multi-tenant master student record with configurable `student_id`, bilingual names (`name_bn`, `name_en`), DOB, gender, blood group, religion, 17-digit birth registration number, photo, present/permanent address, status (`active`, `transferred`, `dropped`, `graduated`, `alumni`, `suspended`), admission date, and special categories (orphan, freedom-fighter quota, disability, stipend).
    - `StudentEnrollment`: Session-specific enrollment linking `Student` to `AcademicSession`, `ClassLevel`, `Section`, `Group`, `Shift`, with unique roll number (`roll_no`) per section/session and enrollment status tracking.
    - `StudentDocument`: Archival document storage with verification workflow.
    - `PreviousSchool`: Previous academic history, board, TC number, and GPA.
    - `StudentIDCard`: Digital/physical ID cards with unique card numbers, JSON QR verification payloads, and RFID/NFC UID mapping.
    - `PromotionBatch` & `PromotionItem`: Academic session progression engine supporting preview, automatic/manual review, atomic commit, and reversible rollback.
    - `TransferCertificate` & `Testimonial`: Official Bangladeshi school clearance and passing certificate records with gap-free sequence numbering (`TC-YYYY-XXXXX`, `TST-YYYY-XXXXX`).
    - `DropoutRecord` & `AlumniProfile`: Dedicated tracking for student attrition and graduated student network.
  - Business Rules & Services:
    - `intake.py`: `admit_from_application` completing the Task 05 bridge to transform admitted applicants into active students and enrollments.
    - `roll_assigner.py`: Configurable automated roll numbering (alphabetical by English/Bangla name, student ID order) with temporary roll offsets preventing unique constraint collisions.
    - `promotion.py`: End-to-end bulk promotion pipeline (`preview_promotion` → `apply_promotion` → `rollback_promotion`) with roll collision prevention and atomic rollback.
    - `transfer.py`: TC issuance workflow transitioning student status to `transferred`, closing active enrollment, and generating official PDF.
    - `bulk_import.py`: Excel spreadsheet parser (`openpyxl`) supporting dry-run validation with row-by-row error reporting and atomic multi-row creation.
    - `id_card_service.py`: Automated student ID card issue and renewal, active card replacement, and badge PDF rendering.
  - Bangla PDF Engine (`apps/core/pdf/`):
    - ReportLab integration registering TrueType Unicode Bengali fonts (`Kalpurush` bundled in `apps/core/fonts/Kalpurush.ttf` with system fallbacks).
    - Templates for Transfer Certificate (ছাড়পত্র), Testimonial (প্রশংসাপত্র), and Student ID Card (আইডি কার্ড).
  - REST API: Endpoints under `/api/v1/students/` for student CRUD, search by Bangla/English name, student ID, and birth reg no, Excel bulk import (`/bulk-import/`), Excel export (`/export/`), student admission intake (`/admit-from-application/`), section roll re-sequencing (`/enrollments/auto-assign-rolls/`), promotion preview/apply/rollback (`/promotions/`), TC issue and PDF download (`/transfer-certificates/`), and Testimonial issue and PDF download (`/testimonials/`).
  - Security: Field-level masking hiding sensitive birth registration numbers and emergency contact phones for unauthorized viewers, role-based permissions (`HasModulePermission("students.view")`, `HasModulePermission("students.manage")`).
  - Architectural Decision Record: ADR-008 (Student Identity Generation, Roll Assignment & Bangla PDF Engine).
- **Task 05**: Admission (Online Application, Test, Merit, Lottery) (`apps/admissions`).
  - Models: `AdmissionCircular` (intake session, class, quota, fee, start/end dates, eligibility criteria), `QuotaRule` (General, Freedom Fighter, Catchment, Special Needs/Disability, Staff Child, Sibling), `Application` (application number `ADM-YYYY-XXXXX`, applicant details, 17-digit birth registration number, guardian contact, status machine), `ApplicationDocument` (photo, birth certificate, marksheet, transfer certificate), `ApplicationPayment` (fee payment transaction, bKash/Nagad/manual, verification), `AdmissionTest` (venue, date, max marks, pass marks), `AdmissionTestResult` (marks obtained, rank, pass/fail), `LotteryDraw` (seeded deterministic PRNG, reproducible candidate order), `MeritList` (merit list generation, approval), `MeritListEntry` (merit rank, waiting rank, quota allocation).
  - Business Rules & Services:
    - `application_workflow.py`: Strict state transition graph (`draft` -> `submitted` -> `fee_paid` -> `under_review` -> `accepted` / `rejected` -> `test_assigned` -> `test_attended` -> `selected` / `waiting` -> `admitted` / `cancelled`), duplicate application prevention using `(circular, birth_reg_no, dob)`, 17-digit BD birth reg number validation, applicant age and minimum GPA validation.
    - `lottery.py`: Auditable deterministic lottery engine utilizing SHA256/Python `Random(seed)` with persistent seed and drawn applicant list for government-mandated lottery admissions.
    - `merit_calculator.py`: Merit scoring and ranking engine with configurable quota reservation caps, handling merit list vs waiting list partitioning.
    - `student_converter.py`: Intake transition service converting `admitted` applications into initial student profile records for Task 06.
  - REST API:
    - Public endpoints under `/api/v1/admissions/public/`: active circulars, multi-step application submission, application status tracking, and downloadable admit card generation.
    - Administrative endpoints under `/api/v1/admissions/`: circulars CRUD, applications management with status transition actions (`/transition/`), document verification (`/verify-document/`), payments review, admission tests and marks entry, lottery draws (`/conduct/`), merit list generation (`/generate/`, `/approve/`), and student intake conversion (`/convert-to-student/`).
  - Security & Integrity: Role-based permissions (`HasModulePermission` for admissions module), public endpoints with throttling and sanitization, audit trail of lottery seed and status transitions.
  - Architectural Decision Record: ADR-007 (Public Prospective Applicant Admission & Deterministic Lottery Engine).
- **Task 04**: Academic Structure (`apps/academics`).
  - Models: `ClassLevel` (medium, order, has_groups), `Group` (Science, Commerce, Humanities), `Section` (class × shift × session with teacher placeholder), `Subject` (code, Bangla/English name, marks components config), `ClassSubject` (session offering, pass rule, credit weight, optional elective flag), `Room` (branch, capacity, type), `Period` (shift, start/end time, order, break flag), `RoutineSlot` (section × weekday × period timetable slot), `SubstituteAssignment` (dated substitution with validation).
  - Database Constraints: `unique_class_code_per_institution`, `unique_class_order_per_medium_institution`, `unique_group_code_per_institution`, `unique_period_order_per_shift`, `unique_room_per_branch`, `unique_section_per_class_shift_session`, `unique_subject_code_per_institution`, `unique_slot_per_section_weekday_period`, partial unique index on room per period, partial unique index on teacher per period, `unique_subject_per_class_group_session`, `unique_substitution_per_slot_date`.
  - Service layer: Timetable conflict detector (`validate_routine_slot_conflicts`, `validate_routine_slots_batch` checking teacher double-booking, room clashes, section clashes, break periods, and calendar weekend warnings), session structure copier (`copy_academic_structure_from_previous_session` supporting `dry_run` preview, section copy, class subject offerings copy, and routine copy).
  - Selectors: Routine queries for sections, teachers, rooms, and a 2D section timetable matrix builder (`build_section_timetable_matrix`).
  - REST API: Endpoints under `/api/v1/academics/` for classes, groups, sections (with `/bulk-create/` and `/{id}/timetable/`), subjects, class subjects, rooms, periods, routine (with `/validate/`), substitute assignments, and `/copy-session/`.
- **Task 03**: Authentication, Users & RBAC (`apps/accounts`).
  - Models: Custom `User` (UUID, institution FK, unique normalized BD phone per tenant, Argon2 hashing, TOTP 2FA fields), `Role`, `UserRole` (with branch scoping), `OTPChallenge`, `UserSession` (trusted device tracking), `LoginAttempt` (brute-force tracking), `PasswordResetToken`.

  - Permissions Framework: `HasModulePermission` DRF class factory, `@require_module_permission` decorator, test helpers (`make_user`, `api_client_as`).
  - REST API: Endpoints under `/api/v1/auth/` for `login/`, `otp/request/`, `otp/verify/`, `refresh/`, `logout/`, `logout-all/`, `password/change/`, `me/`, `sessions/`, `users/`, `roles/`.
  - Architecture Decision Record: ADR-005 (No Public Self-Registration for staff/guardians; restricted to prospective applicants in Task 05).
